Home / How it works

Four entries, start to payout

Perkora doesn't replace your bank card or your UPI app. It watches quietly in the background and credits your passbook whenever an eligible payment goes through.

01 · Create your account

Sign up with your mobile number

Give us your name, mobile number and email. We send a one-time code to confirm the number — no separate app download required.

02 · Link a card or UPI ID

Add what you already pay with

Enter your bank card details or your UPI ID. Card numbers are tokenised on entry — Perkora stores a reference token, not the number itself, and never asks for your PIN, password or OTP for payments.

03 · Spend as usual

Pay exactly the way you do today

Buy groceries, pay a bill, fill fuel or check out online. If the merchant and category match a live cashback rate, the transaction is automatically marked eligible.

04 · Cashback lands in your passbook

Track it like a bank entry

Most cashback reflects in your passbook within 24–48 hours of the payment settling. Every entry shows the date, the category, and the amount credited.

05 · Redeem to your bank

Once you cross ₹100

Move your balance straight to the same bank account linked to your card or UPI ID. No vouchers, no points that expire — just rupees.

Good to know

A few things worth understanding upfront

We never move your money

Perkora only reads transaction metadata needed to match a cashback rate. Payments are always made by you, through your bank or UPI app.

Crediting takes a day or two

Cashback appears once the underlying payment has fully settled with your bank, which is usually within 24–48 hours.

Rates can change monthly

Category cashback rates are reviewed monthly and shown on the cashback categories page before they take effect.

Your first entry is one sign-up away

Create an account, link a card or UPI ID, and let the next payment you make start the passbook.
