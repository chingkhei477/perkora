How rates work

  • Rates apply per calendar month and reset on the 1st.
  • A payment qualifies once it settles with your bank — usually 24–48 hours after you pay.
  • Category caps protect against unusually large one-off payments; everyday spending rarely hits them.
  • Rates are reviewed monthly. Any change is reflected here before it takes effect.
